05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
meet answers.

Still curious? The extension is designed to be predictable, local and easy to undo.

Does AniWebScale upload my videos?

No. Enhancement runs locally on your GPU or, when explicitly enabled, through the local native Windows renderer.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Chromium-based browsers and Firefox builds with the required WebGPU capabilities are supported.

What graphics card do I need?

A modern WebGPU-capable GPU is required. Recent integrated graphics may run lighter Anime4K presets, but a powerful dedicated GPU is strongly recommended for 1080p-to-4K processing, AI modes and frame generation. High-resolution 4x modes can require substantial VRAM, especially when combined with frame generation.

Does it work with Crunchyroll, Netflix and protected video?

Crunchyroll, Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and similar protected services require the Native Windows renderer; WebGPU cannot read protected frames. AniWebScale never bypasses DRM, so individual services may still block capture even with Native enabled.

What is included for free?

Everything. Anime4K real-time presets, the AI upscaling modes (ArtCNN, ACNet, ARNet), the optional Native Windows renderer and motion-aware frame generation are all available at no cost. There is no paid tier.

How do I get support?

Reach out through the support page. AniWebScale runs locally, so most issues are settings or hardware related and easy to resolve.
