2. Website data

When you visit this website, the hosting provider may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser type, requested path, timestamp and response status. This data is necessary to deliver and secure the website. This version of the site does not include advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.

3. Extension data

AniWebScale processes video frames locally on your device through WebGPU or the optional local native renderer. The extension does not upload video frames to this website and does not include telemetry. Browser storage is used for your selected enhancement settings and, where applicable, native renderer site permissions.

4. Payments through Stripe

When you choose a paid Pro plan, you are redirected to Stripe-hosted Checkout. Stripe processes the payment details required to complete the transaction. This website receives transaction metadata such as the selected plan, payment status, Stripe customer or subscription identifiers and the email attached to the Checkout Session. Card details are not handled by this server.

Payment processing is provided by Stripe. Review Stripe's privacy policy for details about its processing and international transfers.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website stores only your light/dark theme preference in browser local storage. No analytics cookies are set by this site. Stripe may use necessary technologies on its hosted Checkout and customer portal pages under its own policies.

6. Retention

Technical server logs should be retained only as long as needed for security and operations. Stripe transaction and invoice records may be retained for contractual, accounting and tax obligations. Configure your hosting and Stripe retention practices to match the laws applicable to your business.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or export personal data and to object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority. Contact to make a request.
